rookie guide

If you're new to lan/demopartys, or even if there's just been a long time since you last been to one, you should read on to know what this is and what you should bring to enjoy id2003 to the fullest.

You don't really have to bring your computer to the party place, you could just come and check out the action, take part in some of the other 'non-pc' compos or even just come to watch the compos on the big-screen, but let's face it, people that bring their computers are the ones that get the most out of events such as this one. Hook up your network cable and you're up'n'runnin' with everyone else at the party. You can play multiplayer games, swap info, and if we're lucky enough even surf the web at high speed. So let's go over a few things that you'll need to come to the party for 3 days:

- Your computer (CPU, monitor, keyboard and mouse);

- Headphones (you can't bring speakers);

- Power extension (must have ground connection);

- All the software you need;

- Sleeping bag (don't want to sleep on the floor do ya?!) and a tent if you want to camp in the garden outside the hall;

- Some food (we will have a snack-bar open during day time, and there are also plenty of places in the area to buy food from);

And that's pretty much it. Don't forget these 'essentials' and you'll have a great time at id2003 ;)

InérciaDemoparty 2003 — 25th till 28th July 2003, Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal.

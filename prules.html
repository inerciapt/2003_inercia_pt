party rules

Here are some rules we find important to set. We don't want to make Inérciademoparty2003 a big mess, do we? :)

PARTY RULES

All PORTUGUESE laws apply at id2003. This includes software piracy, drugs, public safety, weapons usage, alcohol and so on. If you have any doubt, just ask one of the Organizations members.

Alcohol and Tobacco

To bring alcohol inside the party place is forbidden. You cannot enter if you're intoxicated either.

You're not allowed to smoke in the party place. If you want to do so there's plenty of fresh air just outside the entrance.

Software Piracy

Software piracy is illegal in Portugal. We will not be complacent with such activities and it may lead to your expulsion from id2003.

Electrical equipment

It's forbidden to connect any household equipment to the electricity plugs in the party place. This includes fridges, microwaves, toasters, and hair dryers.

Music loudness

We strongly recomment that you manage to get your own headphones. Loudspeakers are not permitted.

Damages to computers and to the party place

Needless to say that violence and vandalism will not go unpunished, either to the party place or to other party participantes. You are responsible for your own actions but, if anything happens, by accident, contact any organization member as soon as possible.

Entering id2003

In order to attend id2003 you must have in your possession a ticket, which may be reserved through our sign up page until July 23rd 2003 at 12:00 and bought at the party place. You can either buy a ticket which grants you a place for your computer or one without a place (both will cost you 15€). These 72 hours passes will give you full access to the party place and to all competitions. After July 23rd you can buy your ticket at the doors for 20€.

There is also a 24 hours pass, available at the doors for 5€.

Girls get in for free - you can buy your "Butterfly Pass" at the doors! - unless they bring their pc (for which they have to get a 72 hours pass, either through the website or at the doors) or want to participate in any compo (for which they must acquire a 24 hours pass for the last day, for the prize giving ceremony).

Finally...

We are not responsible for missing or damaged equipment and personal goods, so be sure to take care of your own things.

We reserve the right to cancel a visitor's participation if any of these rules are violated or if the visitor disturbs other participants, threatening the safety of id2003.

Everything about this id2003 is digitall BUT the rules. 120 visitors, plus their personal computers, for 72 hours is not easy to handle and therefore we ask you, above all, to use good sense. Disrespect to these rules can lead to your expulsion from id2003. The rules are for your own safety and benefit, don't forget about this and this party will be excellent.

InérciaDemoparty 2003 — 25th till 28th July 2003, Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal.

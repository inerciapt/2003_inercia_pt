general information

This year's edition of InérciaDemoparty will happen during the last weekend of July, from 25th till 28th. The place chosen is Ginásio Clube de Mafamude, a sports infrastructure in the heart of the city of Vila Nova de Gaia. For more info click here.

You can sign up already since a few weeks in this website (click on the 'sign up' link at the menu) but you only pay your ticket at the entrance. Sign up it's only possible until 48 hours before the party starts, which means July 23rd 12:00. After that, you can only buy your ticket at the doors, where will be more expensive.

Here are the prices available for id2003:

Online tickets (using the "sign up" form on this website)

  • 72h pass - 15€ (with or without computer)

Tickets at the door

  • 72h pass - 20€ (with or without computer)
  • 24h pass - 5€ (without computer)
  • Butterfly pass (for girls without computer) - Free!

In order to participate in any compo (except the fun compos) you must vote in the compo you sign up to, which means that you must be present during the compo (check the timetable). You also have to get a 24h pass (girls, you too) for the day of the compo. With that ticket you can come in for free on Monday, 28th from 9am on, so you can be at the prize giving ceremony.

If you have any questions don't hesitate in contacting us.

InérciaDemoparty 2003 — 25th till 28th July 2003, Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal.

demoscene compos

pc demo

No hard-drive writing, no more than 10 minutes (or will be stopped), no more than 20Mb (20.971.520 bytes), but please, optimize for size. must exit on 'Esc' or Alt-F4. No pure video-players please. Graphics card on the 2ghz 512RAM compomachine will be dx9 compatible. If your demo wont run properly on our config, we allow you to bring your own machine and run it there as long as it is win32 compatible. For other PC OS's you need to bring your machine and it will be entered on the other platforms compo.

intro 64k

Same rules as for the pc demo compo, only extra rule is that size limit must be below 64kb. (65.536 bytes - the zip or the exe, whichever is smaller).

intro 4k

Same rules as for the pc demo compo, only extra rule is that size limit must be below 4kb. (4096 bytes - the zip or the exe, whichever is smaller)

other platforms

Same timelimit as the pc demo, but the compo machine will vary. Please contact us in advance if you cannot bring your exotic hardware with you, alternatively we can show an .avi, or present it using an emulator, eitherway, you must deliver the rom / binary files.

professional music

Maximum playtime of 5 minutes. If it's longer, it will be faded out. Formats accepted: Mp3 at 160 kbps or VBR HQ and Ogg HQ.

tracked music

Maximum playtime of 5 minutes. If it's longer, it will be faded out. No more than 1Mb (1.048.576 bytes). Formats accepted: mod, s3m, xm, it (will be played in ModPlug Player) and rns (will be played in Renoise).

fast chiptune

Maximum playtime of 5 minutes. No more than 16kb zipped. Will be played in ModPlug Player, so use mod, s3m, it or xm formats. Alternatively you can deliver a SID or AHX or other lo-fi sound format, bring your prefered player with you if so.

Participants will be given a theme and will have 1h30 to make the music :)

This compo will be held simultaneously and in cooperation with EUSKAL in Spain, LCP in Sweden and Syndeecate in the Check Republic.

freestyle graphics

Maximum resolution at 800x600x24bit. Must state tools used. Must present 5 picture stages (for pre-selecting purpouses). Pictures must be named *_(01-05).xxx and included in a zip file named *.zip. Will be shown using latest ACDSee. Png, Jpg ou Gif formats please.

fast gfx

Given a topic only revealed right before the compo takes place, the participant will have a timelimit of one hour and a half to create and deliver the graphics entry. Same accepted fileformats as the freestyle graphics compo.

This compo will be held simultaneously and in cooperation with EUSKAL in Spain, LCP in Sweden and Syndeecate in the Check Republic.

wildcompo

No more than 100Mb (104.857.600bytes) or 10 minutes long. DivX and xvid (open source) codec prefered. No 'exotic' video or audio codecs please. To be submited in a CD with video and info file only.

webdesign

Must work on iexplorer. No bigger then 5Mb (5.242.880 bytes). Maximum resolution at 1024x768x24bit. Must state tools used (plug-ins if any).

game compos

quake III ffa (free for all)

Simple Quake III Free For All rules. Winner takes all. Games will have a maximum of 8 players. Maps will be randomly chosen from the original Quake III maps. Players will be randomly grouped too.

quake III urban terror

Team Deathmatch. Teams of 4, 20 minute rounds. Two teams encounters, direct elimination. Opponents selected randomly.

counter strike

5 vs 5 means just that, team work. Two teams encounters, direct elimination. Opponents selected randomly.

strarcraft broodwar

No teams and no alliances during gameplay. Games will have a maximum of 6 players. Maps and opponents will be randomly chosen from StraCraft Brood War map set (yes, they include the regular StarCraft Maps).

nostalgia trip

A super secret old classic game playing marathon is beeing planned. The idea is to get everyone starting at same time and see who can get biggest highscore/highest level on the game. Last man standing style.

game to be voted online

Use this poll to vote for the game you want to play at inérciademoparty 2003. Rules for this game will follow as soon as the result of the poll is known.

fun compos

chiptune dance

A set of chiptunes will be played, and the contestants will have to dance accordingly to it. The goal is to be as serious as possible, but funny as hell. The jury will include graduated dancers from the CODARTS - University for Dance and Music in Rotterdam, the Netherlands.

throwing olympics

We will organize a throwing olympics consisting of several diferent categories, such as CD and diskette and maybe something more solid. Due to space limitations, precision will be judged instead of distance. More rules will follow.

top

All entries on demoscene compos will be subjected to pre-selection. The organization reserves the right of not showing material that we find offensive in any way or with explicit or racist contents.

All entries on demoscene competitions MUST BE ORIGINAL WORK. A ripped sample or photo mangled and twisted into something different is acceptable, copyright infringments are not.

You or someone representing you must vote on the competition where your entry participated. These votes will constitute a jury which wil have 50% voting power against the remaining 50% from the public voting. Inercia staff members therefor cannot vote unless participating on the competition with an entry.

Voting will begin one hour after each compo ends, respectively. After that, you can vote until 9 am on Monday, 28th. The results will be known during the prize giving ceremony, at 11 am.

All entries will be freely distributed after the end of the event, in this site and in the scene.org ftp.
